Official findings — UTC−5/+2Y
The anomaly originates beneath the Pharmaprix at the intersection of Ste-Catherine and St-Laurent. Researchers are unsure why. The building was previously a Jean Coutu. This has not been ruled out as a factor.
Montreal is 730 days ahead of all surrounding municipalities. Longueuil, Laval, and Brossard are unaffected. Laval is in 2025. No one is surprised.
Residents who drove to Brossard and back in one day have reportedly aged two years in transit. Most did not notice until they checked their hairline.
The federal UTC−5/+2Y designation makes Montreal the only city on Earth with both an hourly and a yearly time offset. The ISO says it is "revising its framework as a matter of urgency."
Weather is identical in both timelines to four decimal points of precision. Scientists describe this as "the most unsettling finding of the entire investigation."
